DSA Operational Principles

FIFO

Kitchen Queue (LinkedOrderQueue)

New tickets enqueue at the Rear. The chef always dequeues from the Front. Both operations operate in O(1) time without java.util collections.

LIFO

Cart Undo Mechanism (LinkedCartActionStack)

Every quantity modification pushes a snapshot to top. Clicking Undo pops the top action and restores previous state in O(1) time.

STATE

Single-Active Station Workflow

Orders advance strictly through PREPARING → READY → COMPLETED. No second order can begin until the active ticket finishes.
